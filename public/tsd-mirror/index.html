These stories are inspired by the real experiences of young people shared with us at Rosie’s Place, stories of challenge, courage, and searching for doors that lead away from harm and toward safety and dignity.

These stories contain material that some readers may find confronting and unsettling. Words or images may bring up feelings of sadness or discomfort, especially for people who have been subjected to sexual harm.
